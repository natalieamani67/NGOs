Our Mission & Regulatory Vision

Advancing employee protections under Kenyan industrial frameworks.

Constitutional Representation

The Kenya Union of Commercial Food & Allied Workers acts as a legal advocate balancing corporate structures with fair working conditions. We monitor factories, shipping centers, and food processing lines nationwide.

By formalizing structured Collective Bargaining Agreements (CBAs), we establish protective floors for medical coverage, overtime scaling, and physical health configurations.
